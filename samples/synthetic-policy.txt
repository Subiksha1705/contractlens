ACME CORPORATE CONTRACT POLICY & STANDARD GUIDELINES

1. NOTICE PERIODS
Standard renewal notice periods must be at least ninety (90) days prior to contract expiration. Any contract with an auto-renewal notice requirement of less than 90 days is a policy mismatch and must be flagged for review.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Maximum acceptable payment terms are net forty-five (45) days. Contracts requiring payment within 30 days are acceptable but require CFO signoff if over $100k.

3. GOVERNING LAW
Acceptable governing jurisdictions are Delaware or New York only.

4. MAINTENANCE NOTICE
Vendor maintenance or downtime windows must be communicated with at least five (5) business days advance written notice. Vague terms such as "reasonable notice" do not meet corporate compliance standards.
